L554: {{#var:picture-resize}}
L555: Once you've ordered the appropriate license for yourself, you can get a crafting table (as well as the 【184†forge】) from Bado after you have activated the 2 central switches to get to the boss room in the 【185†Water Ruins】, which you can place wherever you so choose. Please note that the option for the license will not appear until after you've talked to 【186†Bado】 and he has stated that it's time for you get a crafting table.
L556: Crafting is used to make armor and accessories, and you can get the recipe bread them by eating the accessory bread sold by 【187†Porcoline】. You can also make items without a recipe, if you so choose.
L557: Type  Description  Obtaining
L558: 【614†Image†therunefactory.fandom.com】 Accessory Bread  A magic bread that teaches you new recipes. You can learn one (simple) recipe appropriate to your skill.  Sold by 【187†Porcoline】
L559: 【615†Image†therunefactory.fandom.com】 Accessory Bread+  A nicely baked Accessory Bread. Teaches you a difficult recipe appropriate to your skill.  Found inside 【188†Sharance Maze】
L560: When you use the crafting table, you will be given the option to make armor, shields, headgear, shoes, accessories, or to upgrade armor.
L561: 
L562: A number only require a type of item, and any item that falls into this type may be used.
