Once you've ordered the appropriate licenses for yourself, you can get a chemistry set from Jones or Nancy, which you can place wherever you so choose. The chemistry set is used to make medicines, nutrients, and ingredients. You can get the recipes for them by eating the medicine bread sold by Porcoline. You can also make items without a recipe if you so choose.

 | Medicine Bread  | A magic bread that teaches you new recipes. You can learn one (simple) recipe appropriate to your skill.  | Obtained: Porcoline's Kitchen
 | Medicine Bread+  | A nicely baked Medicine Bread. Teaches you a difficult recipe appropriate to your skill.  | Obtained: Sharance Labyrinth

Chemistry (also known as Pharmacy in Rune Factory series) in Rune Factory 5 is to make medicines, nutrients, ingredients or to give as gifts. To get started at Chemistry, accept the request "Buy Chemistry Set" at the Task Board just outside Rigbarth Outpost. The protagonist will need to pass a test from St. Eliza (the talking mailbox) at Rigbarth Outpost to obtain the Chemistry License. Once done, head to Studio Palmo to buy your Chemistry furniture. The chemistry table will cost you 1,000G, 30 stone, and 30 lumber.

## Tiny Bandage Clinic

## Information

### Hours

9:00~18:00

### Residents

Jones
Nancy
Dolce

### Connected

Selphia: Housing Area

Tiny Bandage Clinic (病院(びょういん)『ちいさな包帯(ほうたい)』, Byouin "Chiisa na Houtai") is a location in Rune Factory 4.

Jones and Nancy own the Tiny Bandage Clinic in Selphia, with Dolce helping once she has been moved in. If the main character faints, he/she will appear here. Upon waking up, you'll be expected to pay a clinic fee. The fee will increase each time you faint, and go up at a steeper rate depending on the difficulty level you are playing on. (There is a price cap for easy and normal mode, but prices in hard mode can go up as high as you can fill your wallet.) If your spouse is with you when you pass out, you'll get a couple's discount.

The only time you will not pay a fee for fainting is when Nancy is working at the clinic.

You can buy various medicines and, once you unlock it, the chemistry set here.
